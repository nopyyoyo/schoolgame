Final Fantasy V Black Magic Effects - Curated Reconstruction

Each effect folder contains Frame_01.png through Frame_xx.png and an animated GIF.
Frames were reconstructed from the visual order on the supplied reference sheet.
Fire2 was assembled from the separate Top, Middle, and Bottom component rows.
Bolt3 uses the two ring states shown. Other effects follow their left-to-right progression.
Transparent padding and a consistent canvas are used within each effect to prevent GIF jitter.
